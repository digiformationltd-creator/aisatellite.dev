### 1. DISCOVERING TECHNOLOGY & FREE ALTERNATIVES

- **How do I find a free alternative to an expensive commercial tool?**
  Navigate to [`/alternatives`](https://aisatellite.dev/alternatives) or use the search bar at the top of any page. Search for the commercial tool name (e.g. "Datadog" or "Docker Desktop") to view verified open-source and free self-hosted equivalents.
- **What makes an alternative "verified"?**
  Every verified tool on AI Satellite has undergone license inspection (OSI-compliant open source), self-hosting feasibility review, and active maintenance checks. We do not list time-limited free trials as permanent free alternatives.

### 2. USING THE AI PROMPT GALAXY

- **How do I use developer prompts?**
  Visit [`/prompts`](https://aisatellite.dev/prompts) and filter by engineering task (Code Refactoring, Security Audit, SQL Optimization, Architecture).
- **Can I customize prompts before copying?**
  Yes. Prompts support variable fields (`{language}`, `{framework}`, `{task}`). Enter your parameters and click "Copy Prompt" for instant sanitized clipboard export.

### 3. MY ORBIT PERSONAL WORKSPACE

- **What is My Orbit?**
  My Orbit (`/workspace`) is your private developer workspace where you can bookmark tools, save prompts, store code snippets, and organize technical learning projects.
- **Is my workspace data private?**
  Yes. Your workspace is strictly private. It is never indexed by search engines, never shared with other users, and never included in sitemaps.
- **Can I export or delete my data?**
  Yes. From your workspace settings, you can export your entire data vault as a JSON file or execute a complete, permanent account deletion at any time.
