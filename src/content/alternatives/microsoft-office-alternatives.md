### Why Organizations Leave Microsoft Office 365
Rising per-seat enterprise licensing fees and strict compliance demands (e.g. GDPR, data residency) make self-hosted and offline-capable office suites essential.

### Evaluated Alternatives
1. **OnlyOffice**: Modern web and desktop suite with identical OpenXML rendering to MS Office.
2. **LibreOffice**: Mature, time-tested open-source productivity suite supported by The Document Foundation.
3. **DigiOffice Suite**: Verified open-source collaborative workspace package.
