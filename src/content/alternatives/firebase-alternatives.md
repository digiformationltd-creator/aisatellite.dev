### Why Teams Migrate Away from Firebase
Firestore pricing scales on every document read and write. As active users increase, accidental client-side loops can trigger unexpected four-figure monthly cloud bills.

### Verified Open-Source Replacements
1. **Supabase**: Real-time Postgres with auth, edge functions, and storage.
2. **Appwrite**: Complete self-hosted BaaS packaged in clean Docker containers.
3. **PocketBase**: Single-file Go backend with SQLite, realtime subscriptions, and admin dashboard.
