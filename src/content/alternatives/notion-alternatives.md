### Why Teams Migrate Away from Notion
Notion's lack of true offline editing, slow document load times on large workspaces, and third-party data hosting concerns have driven technical founders to local-first tools.

### Top Evaluated Replacements
1. **AppFlowy**: The open-source Notion alternative built with Flutter and Rust.
2. **Obsidian**: Local-first Markdown knowledge base with an unmatched plugin ecosystem.
3. **Logseq**: Privacy-first, open-source outliner with built-in PDF annotation and flashcards.
