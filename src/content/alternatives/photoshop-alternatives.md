### Why Creatives Leave Adobe Photoshop
Continuous subscription price hikes, forced cloud storage lock-in, and restrictive licensing have led professionals and studios to adopt open-source graphic tooling.

### Top Evaluated Replacements
1. **GIMP (GNU Image Manipulation Program)**: The gold standard open-source raster editor with 25+ years of active development.
2. **Krita**: Studio-grade digital painting suite favored by concept artists and visual storytellers.
3. **Photopea**: Free, browser-based web application with near-identical Photoshop shortcuts and native PSD layer compatibility.
