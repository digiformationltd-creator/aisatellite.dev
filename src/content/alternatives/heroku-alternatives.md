### Why Teams Migrate Away from Heroku
Dyno pricing has become cost-prohibitive for indie developers and small agencies. A single modest production stack on Heroku easily costs $50-$150/month compared to $6/month on a Hetzner or DigitalOcean droplet using open-source PaaS.

### Verified Open-Source Replacements
1. **Coolify**: The premier self-hosted PaaS with full GUI, database management, and automatic SSL.
2. **Dokku**: The smallest PaaS implementation powered by Docker and Git push hooks.
3. **CapRover**: Extremely easy to use app and database deployer.
