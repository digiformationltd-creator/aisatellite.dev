### Why Teams Migrate Away from Airtable
Airtable's 50,000 row limits on standard business plans force teams into expensive custom enterprise tiers. Open-source solutions eliminate artificial row limits completely by relying on true relational SQL engines.

### Top Evaluated Replacements
1. **NocoDB**: Lightweight Node.js frontend that transforms any relational DB into a no-code database.
2. **Baserow**: Django and Vue-powered open-source database with real-time websocket sync.
3. **Grist**: Hybrid relational spreadsheet with Python-formula capabilities and strict access rules.
